Privacy Policy

Your data is treated the way we treat our clients' budgets: carefully, transparently, and only for the purpose you gave it to us.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

Brand Vitals is a marketing company based in Cairo, Egypt, working with brands in Egypt, Saudi Arabia and the wider region. It runs Vitals Studio, Vitals Web, Vitals Events, Vitals GO, Vitals Crew and Vitals Recruit. This policy covers brandvitals.io and all of these products.

2. What we collect

  • Website forms: your name, email, phone, company, and what you tell us about your goals and budget.
  • Vitals GO: your workspace, clients, files, comments and approvals. Every approval is saved with the approver's name and time, because that record is part of the service.
  • Vitals Crew: freelancer applications and portfolio pieces, job briefs, proposals, deliveries and review notes.
  • Vitals Recruit: candidate profiles, CVs, applications, screening scores and notes, and the jobs companies post.
  • Usage data: pages visited, device and approximate location, collected only after you accept analytics cookies.

3. How we use it

  • To reply to your request and deliver the service you asked for.
  • To run the products: send review links, record approvals, match freelancers to briefs, and screen applications.
  • To improve the website and our services, using aggregated data.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your clients' or candidates' data to advertise to them.

4. Who sees what

  • Vitals GO: review links are unique to each piece of work. Only people with the link, and your team, can see it.
  • Vitals Crew: portfolios are seen by our reviewers. Clients see a freelancer's profile and the work delivered on their own brief.
  • Vitals Recruit: profiles are not public or searchable. Our screening team reads every application; a company sees your profile only after you apply to its job and pass screening.

5. Legal bases and regional laws

We process personal data in line with Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020 and, for people in the Kingdom, Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law. We rely on your consent, on the contract we have with you, or on our legitimate interest in answering requests you send us.

6. Cookies and tracking

Essential cookies keep the site working (your language and theme). Analytics and advertising tags load only after you accept them in the consent banner, and you can change your choice at any time.

7. Storage and transfers

The website and products run on established cloud providers, which may store data outside Egypt or Saudi Arabia. We choose providers with strong security and contract terms that protect your data.

8. Retention

We keep inquiry data as long as needed to serve you. Workspace, profile and application data stay while your account is active and are deleted when you ask us to, unless the law requires us to keep a record.

9. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data, or withdraw consent, at any time. Email info@brandvitals.io or message us on WhatsApp; we reply within 30 days.

10. Changes and contact

If we change this policy, we update the date at the top. Questions go to info@brandvitals.io.
