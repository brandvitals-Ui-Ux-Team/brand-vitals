01 · How it works

A job board, with one step in the middle.

Profiles and applications work the way you already know. The difference is the screening between them.

For candidates
  1. Build a formal profileExperience, education, skills and your CV, once.
  2. Apply in one stepYour profile goes as it is, with an optional short note.
  3. Hear back in 48 hoursShortlisted, or told why not. Then follow every step.
Browse jobs
For companies
  1. Post the jobOne page: title, salary range and requirements.
  2. We screen everyoneCV, experience and portfolio checked by our team.
  3. Meet the matchesEach with a fit score and a note. Interview, offer, hire.
