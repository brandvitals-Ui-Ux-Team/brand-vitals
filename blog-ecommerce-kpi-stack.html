The KPI stack every e-commerce CEO should watch weekly

ROAS is not enough. The five numbers that actually predict whether your e-commerce growth will hold.

A good ROAS can hide a business that is losing money, and a weak ROAS can belong to one that is growing well. CEOs need a short list of numbers that connect marketing to profit, reviewed every week.

The five numbers

Keep this on one page and review it in the same meeting every week.

  • Blended MER: total revenue divided by total marketing spend.
  • New customer CAC: marketing spend divided by first-time buyers.
  • Contribution margin after ads, shipping and returns.
  • Conversion rate by device, especially mobile.
  • Repeat purchase rate within 90 days.

Why platform ROAS is not on the list

Each platform takes credit for sales it touched, so the totals add up to more than you actually sold. It is useful for optimizing inside the platform, not for deciding how much the business should spend.

Local realities

In Egypt and much of the region, cash on delivery and returns can change the picture completely. Measure delivered revenue, not placed orders, and include failed deliveries in your CAC.

The takeaway

If you only watch one number, watch contribution margin after marketing. It is the one that pays the bills.
