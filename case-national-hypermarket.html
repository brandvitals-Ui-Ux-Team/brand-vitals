The challenge

A national retailer needed to grow three different customer journeys at once: app installs and orders, web orders, and visits to physical branches, each with different tracking and different economics.

What we did

  • Planned media by funnel, with separate goals for app, web and store traffic.
  • Managed more than EGP 10M in media with disciplined budget allocation.
  • Aligned campaigns with seasonal retail moments and offers.
  • Built reporting that showed the whole picture, not three separate stories.

The result

Omnichannel campaigns delivered a 20× return on ad spend across more than EGP 10M in managed media.
