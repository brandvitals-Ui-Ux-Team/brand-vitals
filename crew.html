01 · The edge

100% reviewed. Four gates, every time.

Open marketplaces let anyone in and leave you to sort through hundreds of bids. We do the sorting. A person on our team signs off at every step, with a scorecard and a written reason.

Portfolio review

A senior reviewer scores real past work for craft, not a CV.

Paid skill test

A small real brief, judged on craft and on how well it answers the brief.

Interview

Communication, reliability and how they take feedback. Then a level: Vetted, Pro or Elite.

QA on every delivery

Every file is checked against the brief before the client sees it. Returned work never reaches you.

02 · For clients

Three options, chosen for you, with the reason.

Post a brief. We check scope and budget, open it to vetted talent only, screen every proposal and send you the best three. Each one comes with a note from our reviewer on why they fit.

  • Payment held in escrow, released only when you approve
  • Free replacement if the talent doesn't deliver
  • Shortlist or fully managed: we can run the whole crew

03 · For freelancers and teams

Win on quality, not on the lowest price.

Every brief you see is real, with a checked budget. Your proposal is judged by people who know the craft, and you get feedback either way. Clients pay into escrow before you start, and our fee is charged to the client, not taken from you.

  • Join solo or as a team
  • Levels that grow with every QA-reviewed delivery
  • Paid the moment the client approves

04 · The review team

Nothing reaches a client without passing us.

Our reviewers vet applicants, approve briefs, build shortlists and check every delivery against a five-point checklist: the brief, the brand, sizes and formats, Arabic and English proofreading, and source files.

  • Brand Vitals' own team can bid too, and is reviewed the same way
  • Two returned deliveries in a row trigger a quality review

07 · FAQ

What people ask first.

Nobody joins without passing a portfolio review, a paid skill test and an interview. We screen every proposal before you see it, and we check every delivery against your brief before it reaches you.

You pay the agreed price plus our service fee into escrow when you hire. The talent is paid when you approve the final delivery. If the talent doesn't deliver, we replace them at no cost.

Both. Studios and small crews apply as a team and are vetted as a team. For bigger briefs we can also assemble a crew from several members and manage it for you.
