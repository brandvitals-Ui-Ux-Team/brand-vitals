06 · FAQ

What people ask first.

Posting a job and sending proposals work the way you already know. The difference is the review: nobody joins without passing four gates, we screen every proposal and send you the best three, we QA every delivery, and if a freelancer fails our rules we replace them at our cost without moving your deadline.

Send at least three portfolio pieces. A reviewer scores them, then you get a paid skill test and a short interview. Your account unlocks only when you're approved.

You pay the agreed price plus our service fee into escrow when you hire. The freelancer is paid when you approve the final delivery.

Our in-house team can send a proposal like any member, is clearly labelled, and goes through the same review. The client always chooses.
