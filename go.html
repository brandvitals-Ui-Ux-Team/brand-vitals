02 · The three lights

Every deliverable is always one of three colors.

And Vitals GO knows what to do in each one, so your team doesn't have to chase.

Amber · with the client

Sent on the channel they use. If they go quiet for two days, Vitals GO sends the reminder for you.

Red · changes requested

Notes are pinned on the exact spot of the design or video frame, and every round is counted against what you agreed.

GO

Green · GO

Recorded with name, time and version. Approved posts can publish themselves on their date.
